AI-powered security for
forests & wildlife

WildShield AI analyzes uploaded surveillance footage from forest reserves and flags potential threats - illegal entry, poaching indicators, weapons, traps and human presence in restricted areas. Results are decision support for authorized personnel, never automated accusations.

Detection focus

What the system looks for

Threat categories are configurable and evolve as custom-trained models are added. Every finding is reported with a confidence score and a threat level.

🚪

Illegal entry

Human presence inside monitored or restricted forest zones.

🏺

Poaching indicators

Suspicious activity patterns that may indicate poaching behavior.

⚔️

Weapons

Apparent weapons in footage, flagged for manual review.

🧰

Animal traps

Objects resembling snares or traps near wildlife corridors.

👤

Restricted-area humans

People detected where access is prohibited.

🚚

Vehicles

Vehicles in areas closed to traffic.

🐾

Wildlife presence

Animal detections with species-level ID once a trained model is loaded.

⚠️

Endangered-species risk

Detections co-occurring near protected species raise alert levels.

Pipeline

From footage to report

YOLO object detectionPersons, animals, vehicles, weapons-class objects, traps
Threat analysisConfigurable rules map detections to LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL
Results & timelineDetections table, snapshots, jump-to-timestamp playback
Security reportDownloadable PDF for authorized personnel review
