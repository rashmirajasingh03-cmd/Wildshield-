02

Why outposts need remote eyes

Forest ranges are vast and thinly staffed. Footage arrives from trails, waterholes and corridor edges — and must be judged quickly, calmly and consistently.

WildShield AI is a decision-support console for forest officers. It watches the footage you cannot be watching, and reports one thing: whether an attack to an animal occurred in the frame.

The result is deliberately simple — Attack Detected or No Attack Detected — alongside evidence and a downloadable report for the record. Detection is only the first step; every finding is meant to be verified by authorized personnel before any field action.

The operational pipeline

One continuous path from raw footage to an alerted officer — nothing hidden, nothing invented.

Ingest / 01 Video Surveillance footage is uploaded through the console — mp4, mov, avi, mkv, webm, flv or wmv.
Inference / 02 AI analysis Frames are extracted and passed through the YOLO detection and VideoMAE pipelines.
Assess / 03 Threat detection Detections are reduced to a single threat verdict for the monitored footage.
Respond / 04 Alert Officers see the result immediately and can generate a PDF report for the record.
